Meet Our Stylists

Our hairstylists are friendly, creative, and passionate about helping you look
and feel amazing. Each stylist has their own unique
style and skills, so you’re always in good hands.

Shaniqua

Shaniqua is our top stylist, with many years of experience with braids, wigs, and protective styling. She focuses on neat,
long-lasting hairstyles that suit each client perfect

Nina

Nina enjoys styling natural hair. Treatments, silk presses,
and maintaining healthy hair are some of her specialties to keep your hair strong and shiny.

Charmaine

Charmaine is noted for her unique braiding skills that produce clean-looking hairstyles.
She specializes in knotless braids and cornrows, all of which look neat and professional.

Joe

Joe is known for his precise fade cuts and detailed line-ups.
He works on all details to produce a modern and neat hairstyle.

Gomolemo

Gomolemo loves trying new styles and designs.
From hair patterns to trendy cuts, he brings creativity into every haircut.

Benson

Benson is new to the salon but very experienced and careful. He assists with basic haircuts,
and he keeps improving daily under the supervision of the other staff members.
